Project History

A short fictional timeline showing how the project grew from idea to prototype.

  1. Sep 2023

    Research kickoff

    Initial campus interviews and early field notes. The team mapped high-traffic routes and sketched the first safety-layer concept.

  2. Feb 2024

    Data sprint

    Alex ran a data sprint to collect coordinates and validate node descriptions. The dataset reached its first stable release.

  3. Oct 2024

    Prototype & usability

    First interactive prototype using Leaflet was released to a small test group. Feedback led to improved popups and clearer legend items.

  4. Apr 2025

    Public demo

    The project was shown at a campus demo day. A short demo video was produced to explain the idea to non-technical audiences.
